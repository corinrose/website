I'm a carpenter and project manager who likes figuring out what's actually going on: why a deck is rotting, why a beam is sagging, why a student is stuck. I'll explain what I find in plain terms, then fix it properly or help you do it yourself.

# Building and Renovation

Based in the Burlington, Vermont area.

* **Renovation and restoration**, including homes built before 1900.
* **Structural repair.** Finding the real cause of problems like rot, split or undersized framing, and sagging floors, and fixing them right. I work with structural engineers when a job calls for one.
* **Decks and outdoor structures** built to dry out and last, with open framing and good airflow.
* **Finish carpentry.** Trim, built-in shelving, and custom closets.
* **Custom saunas**, from design through build. See my [build](/build) page for the saunas I worked on at Madbush Falls.
* **Energy efficiency improvements**, such as continuous exterior insulation, that cut operating costs without changing how a space looks.
* **Project management for larger jobs.** Estimating and material takeoffs, crew leadership, and acting as your owner's representative when several contractors are involved.

I'd rather be thorough than fast. You'll know what I found, what your options are, and what each one costs before the work starts.
