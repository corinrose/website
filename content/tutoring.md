# Hi, I'm Corin.

I provide targeted, 1-on-1 remote coaching in **AP Calculus, AP Physics, Computer Science, and Advanced Math**.

My goal isn't just to help students memorize formulas to pass next week's exam—it's to help them build deep, structural intuition for how these concepts work so they can solve complex problems independently.

---

## How I Approach Teaching

Most STEM anxiety comes from treating math and physics like a list of arbitrary rules to memorize. When a concept feels abstract, it becomes exhausting. 

My approach is different. I break down complex theoretical ideas into clear, intuitive mental models. Having spent my career moving between abstract computer science, formal mathematics, startup engineering, and physical carpentry, I bring real-world context to every session.

When a student understands *why* a formula works—and how it connects to physical reality—the confidence follows naturally.
