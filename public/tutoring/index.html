Accelerated STEM Coaching

Oct 13, 2026

Hi, I’m Corin.

I provide targeted, 1-on-1 remote coaching in AP Calculus, AP Physics, Computer Science, and Advanced Math.

My goal isn’t just to help students memorize formulas to pass next week’s exam—it’s to help them build deep, structural intuition for how these concepts work so they can solve complex problems independently.

How I Approach Teaching

Most STEM anxiety comes from treating math and physics like a list of arbitrary rules to memorize. When a concept feels abstract, it becomes exhausting.

My approach is different. I break down complex theoretical ideas into clear, intuitive mental models. Having spent my career moving between abstract computer science, formal mathematics, startup engineering, and physical carpentry, I bring real-world context to every session.

When a student understands why a formula works—and how it connects to physical reality—the confidence follows naturally.

A Bit About My Background

I’ve been teaching and building in one form or another for over a decade:

  • Academic Foundation: Math & CS double-major at Vassar College; MSSM alum (with 5s on AP Calculus BC and AP Physics C).
  • Teaching & Mentorship: Former high school math teacher at Kents Hill School, Google igniteCS program leader, and long-time academic peer tutor.
  • Real-World Application: Co-founded a tech startup as CTO, and spend my days working in carpentry and construction project management here in Vermont.

How It Works & Pricing

I keep my client roster small—capping capacity at 4 students at a time—so I can give each student my full attention and energy.

  • Single 1-on-1 Session: $85 / hour
  • 4-Session Course Bundle: $320 (Recommended for weekly continuity)

Sessions take place over video with an interactive digital whiteboard setup, allowing us to work through problem sets, proofs, and vector diagrams seamlessly in real time.

Scheduling & Cancellation Policy

  • 24-Hour Notice: Life happens, but because time slots are limited, please provide at least 24 hours’ notice if you need to cancel or reschedule a session.
  • Late Cancellations: Sessions canceled with less than 24 hours’ notice are charged in full.
  • Emergencies: Illness and unexpected emergencies are the exception—everyone gets one grace pass per semester for sudden last-minute reschedules.
